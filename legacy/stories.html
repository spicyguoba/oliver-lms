🔥 Consecutive story successes

👨‍👦 Parent Corner

Each story has five comprehension questions. A successful reading means at least 4 correct answers. To unlock the next realm, your child needs 3 successful readings in a row from 3 different stories. One unsuccessful reading resets the streak in that realm to zero. The stories are shuffled and rotate to reduce repetition.

Ways to help

Ask: “What happened first?” “What in the story tells you that?” and “What might happen next?” Encourage him to point to a sentence that supports his answer. Some comprehension questions require a simple inference, not just remembering a fact.

This is recreational and supplementary learning, not a VALLSS or DIBELS assessment. Stored progress can be lost if Safari website data is cleared or the game is opened in private mode or on another device.
